## Interpretation and limits

The version filter added in this head prevents three tested newer array functions (`array_first`, `array_find`, `array_find_key`) from appearing in the tested 7.4 `array_f` responses, including after returning from 8.5. It does not cover every PHP-version-specific builtin: `array_last` appears in both initial and returned 7.4 Worker raw/UI results despite being available in the 8.5 positive control. `str_contains` also appears under 7.4 in the first hash-identified Worker, then is absent under 7.4 after switching away and back. These are observed result patterns; this run does not establish their internal cause or guarantee all annotated builtins are filtered.

This is a focused completion check, not a parser or diagnostics check. `array_is_list`, `json_validate`, named-argument behavior, syntax acceptance and diagnostics were not measured. Do not infer whole-parser PHP 7.4 compatibility or treat missing diagnostics as acceptance. Earlier pre-hash observations in this session were excluded. An initial 8.5 helper run used a literal `\\n` instead of a newline, producing a one-line document and line-0 requests; it is retained in the trace but excluded. Corrected positive controls used the actual two-line document and line-1 requests.
